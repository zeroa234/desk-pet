## Demo character pack `packs/mage/` (not MIT)

`packs/mage/` is a demo asset pack so the app runs out of the box. It is **not** covered by the MIT License:

- The sprite sheets are rendered from a blocky character model made by the author. Its look was inspired by a third-party character design.
- The animations were taken from the Galath and Jenny animation files of a third-party Minecraft mod (Jenny mod) and re-rendered as sprites.

The pack is provided for personal, non-commercial evaluation of the framework only. Do not redistribute it on its own or use it commercially. If you are a rights holder and want it removed, please open an issue.

To ship your own character, replace `packs/mage/` with a pack you have the rights to (format: `docs/pack-format.md`).
